# Tone and Audience Alignment

## Prompt
"Give feedback to an employee who missed a deadline."

## Intended Audience
Junior employee who is anxious and new to the role.

## AI Response
Hi [Employee Name],

I want to follow up on the deadline for [project/task], which was scheduled for [date]. The deliverable wasn’t completed on time, and I want to talk through what happened.

Meeting deadlines is important because it affects team planning and downstream work. When timelines slip, it can put extra pressure on others and delay overall progress.

I’d like to understand what challenges came up and what support or adjustments might help prevent this in the future. Going forward, please communicate early if you anticipate a delay so we can problem-solve together and adjust expectations as needed.

Let’s plan to discuss this and align on next steps.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

## Tone Issues Identified
- Sounded colder than it could be
- Focused more on rules than reassurance

## Prompt Adjustment
"Give supportive, constructive feedback to a new employee who missed a deadline, emphasizing learning and improvement."

## Improved Response
Hi [Employee Name],

I wanted to check in about the deadline for [project/task], which was scheduled for [date]. Since you’re still getting settled into the role, I see this as a good opportunity to learn and make adjustments going forward.

I know there’s a lot to absorb early on, and missed deadlines can happen while you’re getting familiar with processes, priorities, and expectations. That said, timelines are important for keeping work on track across the team.

I’d love to hear what got in the way and what felt unclear or challenging. My goal is to make sure you have the context, tools, and support you need to be successful. Going forward, please don’t hesitate to flag potential delays early — we can always work through solutions together.

You’re making progress, and I’m confident this is something we can improve quickly. Let’s connect and talk through next steps.

Thanks,
[Your Name]

## Insight
Tone must be explicitly specified to avoid misalignment. Also it should ask if the feedback should be given as a conversation or as a written format like an email.
